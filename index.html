Where I come in

Most people come to me at one of two points.

IT SOX Readiness

Getting ready for SOX?

You've raised a round or you're preparing for an IPO. Plenty of people can tell you what's missing. I help you actually put it in place.

  • Where you stand today: A clear picture of what's in scope and what's missing
  • A path forward: What to fix first, what's a quick win, and what can wait
  • Hands-on build: I work with your control owners to design processes that fit how your team works, so they can run them on their own quarter after quarter

IT SOX Program Ownership

Have a program, but no one really owns it?

This happens at mature companies too. Someone's in charge on paper and can tell something's off, but IT controls aren't their specialty. I step in as your program owner, setting direction and keeping things moving all year, without the cost of a full-time hire.

  • Clear direction and leadership: A dedicated owner making judgment calls and setting priorities
  • Hands-on partner: I work directly with your team, whether that's remediating findings or building new, better controls
  • A managed audit relationship: Requests get vetted and handled so your team isn't scrambling every cycle

Not sure where you stand? Whether you need a few hours a week of guidance or hands-on help getting controls in place, a quick conversation is the easiest way to figure it out.
